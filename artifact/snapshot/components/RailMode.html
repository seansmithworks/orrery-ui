Why

  • You ran 9 passes in 3 weeks, 5 of them in the last 8 days, across seansmithdesign, ghostties, desk, vista-sheet and _experiments.
  • Most passes ran freehand. Shotfun drove only 2 of 9. html-review-workflow and impeccable were read in 2 others.
  • No written rubric existed, so each pass got re-briefed by hand. You retyped the brief, assigned tiers and rebuilt a gallery every time.

The plan

  1. Extend shotfun with a Ladder mode, built through /skill-forge:
    • Tiers use your words. Every card names the one rule it breaks.
    • Count defaults to 10 split evenly, and you override freely.
    • Two carry-forward modes: "push them all forward" and "ladder from #11".
    • Outputs go to a durable gitignored folder, never /tmp.
  2. Arm on your next real pass in Pencil or HTML. The evidence is cards with tier and breaks filled in.
  3. Measure with your picks, not a judge. Log rounds-to-pick and the pick's tier per run.
